# 1. Brand Foundation

## Where It All Began

**“A little bird. A bigger idea.”**

Every name has a story, and ours began with a little bird called Hudhud.

Inspired by the Hoopoe, we were drawn to its distinctive character and the curiosity it sparked in us. It reminded us that inspiration can be found in unexpected places, often when we take a moment to look beyond the obvious.

That idea reflects the way we approach creativity. We believe great ideas begin with asking questions, exploring different perspectives and discovering possibilities others might overlook.

Over time, Hudhud became more than the inspiration behind our name. It came to represent the creative spirit we wanted our studio to embody: curious in thought, thoughtful in approach and always open to exploring something new. And that's how Hoopoe Design came to life, built around the belief that every great idea begins with curiosity.

**Curious by nature. Creative by instinct.**

## Our Creative Compass

### Stay Curious
We believe great ideas begin with curiosity. We ask questions, explore possibilities and look beyond the obvious because there's always something new to discover.

### Think Thoughtfully
Good design is never accidental. We give every idea the attention it deserves, make intentional decisions and consider the little details that make a meaningful difference.

### Challenge the Ordinary
We're not here to follow the obvious route. We question conventions, explore different perspectives and look for fresh ways to turn familiar ideas into something unexpected.

### Create with Purpose
We believe creativity should do more than look good. Every design should have a reason to exist, communicate something meaningful and leave a lasting impression.

## The Way We Speak

**“A voice that's curious, thoughtful and full of character.”**

We communicate with curiosity and confidence, keeping things friendly, clear and approachable. Our words reflect how we think and create: thoughtful when it matters, playful when there's room and always genuine.

### Curious, not complicated
We ask questions, spark ideas and make people think.

### Thoughtful, not formal
We communicate with intention without sounding corporate or distant.

### Playful, not silly
We enjoy a little wit and personality without losing meaning.

### Confident, not loud
We let our ideas speak for themselves, without unnecessary claims or exaggeration.

**We speak like creatives, think like explorers and keep things human.**

# 2. Logo Design

## Logo Concept

### The Hudhud Crest
The fan-shaped crest is inspired by the distinctive feathers of the Hoopoe, giving the identity a recognisable symbol connected to its name.

### The Signature Orange Feather
The orange centre feather adds warmth and character to the otherwise minimal identity.

### The Crest Within the Wordmark
The crest is integrated directly into the wordmark, making the bird an essential part of the identity rather than a separate decorative element.

### Custom Letterforms
The rounded lowercase lettering gives the wordmark a friendly, approachable character while keeping it distinctive and memorable.

# 4. Typography

## Heading Fonts

**Typeface:** Bricolage Grotesque  
**Role:** Primary Font

### Type Rules

**Heading Style**

Use Bold or SemiBold weights for main headings and section titles. Keep headings expressive, confident and easy to recognise.

**Supporting Style**

Use Medium for subheadings and smaller titles to maintain a clear visual hierarchy.

**Tone**

Distinctive, expressive, modern and slightly playful. Let the font bring character to the identity.

**Avoid**

Avoid excessive letter spacing, overly condensed styles or using too many different weights. Keep long paragraphs out of this typeface.

## Body Fonts

**Typeface:** Manrope  
**Role:** Secondary Font

### Type Rules

**Body Style**

Use Regular weight for paragraphs and descriptions, with 14–16px sizing and approximately 1.5–1.6 line height.

**Supporting Style**

Use Medium or SemiBold for labels, captions, navigation and emphasis.

**Tone**

Clean, modern, minimal and approachable. Prioritise readability and consistency.

**Avoid**

Avoid using ExtraBold for long paragraphs, excessive letter spacing or mixing too many weights within the same layout.
